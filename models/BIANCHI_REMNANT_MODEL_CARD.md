# Model Card — White-Hole Remnant Constraint Case Study

## Identity

- **Model name:** White holes as long-lived remnants after black-hole evaporation
- **Primary sources:** `BianchiEtAl2018`, supported by `HaggardRovelli2014`
- **Model class:** loop-inspired effective/remnant scenario
- **Role in this project:** first non-holographic gravitational constraint case study

## Geometry and dynamics

The source scenario describes a black hole that forms, evaporates, undergoes a non-perturbative transition to a white-hole phase, and then decays slowly as a low-mass remnant with a large finite interior. The supporting Haggard--Rovelli construction supplies a classical exterior metric outside a finite quantum region for a black-to-white transition.

The source literature does **not** provide a complete microscopic quantum channel from arbitrary infalling states to all late radiation and remnant degrees of freedom. Global unitarity is therefore an assumption to test conditionally, not a derived channel property.

Required before a dynamical simulation:

- one explicit effective metric and parameterization;
- transition and lifetime law;
- hypersurfaces defining the intermediate and final states;
- treatment of Hawking backreaction;
- state-space or entropy prescription for the white-hole interior;
- coupling that produces final decay radiation.

## Quantum systems and algebras

- **Input `X`:** a finite code subspace representing infalling matter and selected black-hole microstate degrees of freedom.
- **Reference `R`:** an external purifying system used only to define preservation and recoverability.
- **Radiation `A`:** all asymptotic Hawking radiation plus any white-hole decay radiation included up to a declared time cut.
- **Retained sector `B`:** white-hole interior/remnant degrees of freedom on that time cut.
- **Environment `E`:** any degrees omitted by the effective description, including unresolved quantum geometry.
- **Observer algebra:** the algebra generated by asymptotic radiation modes available to a specified observer.

The complete map `X -> A tensor B tensor E` may be taken as isometric only under an explicit global-unitarity assumption. Reduced maps are generally non-isometric.

## First valid constraint

For a pure state on `RAB`, if the retained sector has effective dimension `d_B`,

\[
I(R:B)\leq 2\min\{S(R),\log_2d_B\},
\]

and

\[
I(R:A)\geq\max\{0,2S(R)-2\log_2d_B\}.
\]

The first model calculation will therefore be parametric:

1. infer or assume an effective remnant entropy cap `S_B^max`;
2. convert it to `d_B <= 2^{S_B^max}` when a finite-dimensional interpretation is justified;
3. calculate the minimum radiation mutual information compatible with global purity;
4. identify parameter regions in which the remnant can or cannot contain all reference correlations.

This constrains correlation storage only. Recovery fidelity from radiation requires an additional decoupling or approximate-correctability assumption.

## Falsifiable outputs

- A required remnant entropy or dimension for a declared input code subspace.
- A proof that a proposed finite remnant cannot contain all reference correlations under stated bounds.
- An underdetermination result if the source model supplies no usable state-space bound.
- A list of extra microscopic assumptions required to construct a decoder.

## Prohibited claims

- Large interior volume automatically implies a sufficiently large quantum information capacity.
- Long lifetime automatically implies high storage fidelity.
- Small external mass fixes the internal Hilbert-space dimension.
- A causal diagram determines the Kraus operators.
- Island or AdS/CFT results apply without an explicit duality map.

## Claim coordinates

- **A — Assumptions:** global purity or specified environment, finite input code subspace, declared effective remnant dimension/entropy, defined asymptotic radiation algebra.
- **M — Model:** Bianchi et al. white-hole remnant scenario with Haggard--Rovelli transition geometry.
- **Q — Quantity:** mutual information cap, coherent information where defined, recovery fidelity only after a decoder exists.
- **O — Observer:** asymptotic radiation observer at a specified time cut.
- **E — Evidence:** primary-source geometry plus analytic bounds and reproducible simulations.
- **P — Publication state:** selected case study; not yet theorem-ready.

## Decision

**Include as the first non-holographic constraint case study.**

The next required calculation is a source-grounded parameter table for remnant mass, interior geometry, lifetime, entropy claims, and missing microscopic inputs. If no defensible state-space bound can be extracted, the publishable result becomes a precise underdetermination statement rather than a simulated channel.
